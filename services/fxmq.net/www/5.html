Cloudycloud.fxmq.net · Nextcloud 34

the cuddle cloud ♡

Cloudy is the softest service of all — it holds all your files, photos, calendars & contacts and never lets anyone else peek. It even does little video calls with its friend Talk! Your phone can sync with it from anywhere (and it sends its letters through the lab's own post office, how sweet).

Kuma-bearkuma.fxmq.net · Uptime Kuma

the official worrier 💗

Kuma-bear (its name literally means "bear"!) is the sweetest worrywart: it watches every single friend in the squad and does little heartbeat check-ups all day. If anyone ever feels unwell, the whole world can see it on its colouring page — green for happy, red for "needs a hug".

Blockymc.fxmq.net · Minecraft corner

the gaming sleepover host 🎮

Blocky throws the best sleepovers — open a tab, hit /play, and you're building castles in Minecraft 1.8.8 right in the browser — no install, no sign-up, just fun. Big-kid Java players (1.7.10 to newest) can hop in too on port 25565. The grown-up control room (PufferPanel) lives at /panel and keeps registration closed so only invited friends play.

Momomail.fxmq.net · Roundcube + mailserver

the homelab post office 💌

Momo delivers letters for your very own domain — read them in the pretty webmail, or with any real mail app via SMTP & IMAP. It stamps every envelope with DKIM, SPF & DMARC so nobody can pretend to be you, and it wears the same shiny Let's Encrypt necklace as this very page. All the other squad members write to you through Momo — that's how much they trust them!

webmailsmtp 25/465/587imap 993anti-spoof stamps
visit momo! ☆ home repo adopted from docker-mailserver ↗
everyone welcome! 🌍mailbox holders only

Tailytail.fxmq.net · the secret treehouse

the secret clubhouse 🔒

Taily is the coziest — a secret treehouse only the family (Tailnet) can find. It's not in any public phonebook, and if a stranger knocks, the door just answers "403, sorry!". Inside there's a map of the whole squad at / and, at /ttyd, a magic window where you can talk to the house itself. If you can see it — you're family. 💜
