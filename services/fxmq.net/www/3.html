the planes of the map

Edge plane 1

Cloudflare terminates public TLS, filters bots and answers DNS. mail + mc are grey-cloud — their protocols aren't HTTP(S).

Door plane 2

Caddy 2.11 (custom image, caddy-dns/cloudflare) reverse-proxies every vhost and issues per-vhost Let's Encrypt certs via DNS-01.

Host plane 3

One Debian VPS, eleven docker containers on one net bridge — every container healthchecked and RAM-capped.

Mesh plane 4

Tailscale ties the operator's devices (and a 1 TB storage VPS) to the box; tail.fxmq.net lives only inside it.
